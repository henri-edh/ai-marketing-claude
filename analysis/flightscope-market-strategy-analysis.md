## 1. BUSINESS MODEL ANALYSIS (Score: 75/100)

### Strengths
- **Clear Revenue Diversification**: Hardware (5 price points), software upgrades, simulation bundles, accessories, and pre-owned markets
- **Premium Positioning**: X3C at $13,495 anchors brand as professional-grade technology
- **Trade-In Program**: Creates upgrade cycles and customer retention (Mevo+ → Mevo Gen2, Mevo → Mevo Gen2)
- **Bundle Strategy**: Simulator packages ($6,897+) increase average order value (AOV)

### Weaknesses
- **Limited Recurring Revenue**: Software upgrades are one-time purchases, not subscriptions
- **No Visible Loyalty Program**: Missing customer retention mechanism beyond product upgrades
- **Heavy Hardware Dependency**: Revenue concentrated on durable goods with long replacement cycles
- **Pre-Owned Market Untapped**: Certified pre-owned program exists but not actively promoted

### Recommendations
1. **Launch Subscription Tiers**: $29/month for advanced analytics, $49/month for AI coaching insights
2. **Create Loyalty Program**: "FlightScope Pro" membership with early access, exclusive content, discounts
3. **Expand Pre-Owned Marketplace**: Create dedicated marketplace for used units with certification

## 2. PRICING STRATEGY ANALYSIS (Score: 78/100)

### Current Pricing Structure
- **Entry Level**: Mevo Certified Pre-Owned ($299)
- **Mid-Tier Sale**: Mevo+ ($1,099, was $2,299) - 52% discount clearance
- **Mid-Tier Regular**: Mevo Gen2 ($1,299)
- **Premium**: FlightScope X3C ($13,495, was $14,995) - 10% discount
- **Software Upgrades**: Pro Package + Face Impact ($850, was $1,499) - 43% discount
- **Simulator Bundles**: $6,897+

### Pricing Strategy Type
- **Value-Based Pricing**: Premium positioning for professional accuracy
- **Competitive Pricing**: Mevo+ at $1,099 competes directly with Foresight GC3 ($3,500+) and SkyTrak+
- **Psychological Pricing**: Heavy discounts create urgency ("last remaining stock", "lowest price ever")

### Issues Identified
1. **Price Confusion**: Mevo+ clearance ($1,099) vs Mevo Gen2 ($1,299) - only $200 difference for newer model
2. **Discount Dependency**: Heavy discounts may train customers to wait for sales
3. **Software Pricing**: One-time fees leave recurring revenue on table

### Recommendations
1. **Reposition Mevo+**: Raise to $1,499 or position as "Mevo+ Lite" with clear differentiation
2. **Subscription Software**: Convert Pro Package to $9.99/month or $99/year recurring
3. **Dynamic Pricing**: Implement time-based offers instead of permanent discounts
